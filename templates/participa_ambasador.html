Become an Ambassador

Apply to be an Ambassador

Do you want to bring Lichenary to your country?
Then become one of the first official Lichenary ambassadors.

Ambassadors are our extensions worldwide — team leaders, local voices, catalysts for change. If you are motivated to coordinate, inspire, and organize people, this role is for you.

What does an ambassador do?

  • Establishes a local Lichenary branch in their country or region
  • Translates materials and adapts guides to local realities
  • Coordinates volunteering and mapping activities in their area
  • Recruits volunteers, organizes training sessions and events
  • Maintains direct contact with the central Lichenary team
  • Contributes to expanding the ecological and educational impact of the project

Who can become an ambassador?

  • Anyone aged 14 or older
  • Passionate about environment, education, and science
  • Has organizational, communication, and initiative skills
  • Knows English
  • Can work online and has regular internet access

What will you receive?

  • Official recognition from the Lichenary team
  • Ongoing support from the central team
  • An international network of young ambassadors like you
  • The chance to spark real change in your community
  • Certificates, recommendations, and opportunities for joint projects

How to apply?

Fill in the form on the top of this page, or send us a message on our Instagram page @lichenary.ro, tell us about yourself and why you want to represent Lichenary in your country.
We will guide you step by step to build your own chapter.

You don't have to change the world alone.
But you can start with your city.
